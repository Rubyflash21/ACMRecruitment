# Students Performance — Insight Architect

## Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Students Performance Dataset using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

The goal is to use visualizations to identify patterns, relationships, and trends in student performance across mathematics, reading, and writing.

## Visualizations

### 1. Math Score Histogram

The histogram shows the distribution of students' mathematics scores and helps identify where scores are concentrated and how widely performance varies.

### 2. Average Score Bar Chart

The bar chart compares the average scores for mathematics, reading, and writing. Reading has the highest average score, while mathematics has the lowest average.

### 3. Score Distribution Box Plot

The box plot compares the distributions of the three subjects, showing their medians, spread, and potential outliers.

### 4. Reading vs. Writing Scatter Plot

The scatter plot examines the relationship between reading and writing performance. It shows a strong positive relationship, meaning students with higher reading scores generally tend to have higher writing scores.

### 5. Correlation Heatmap

The heatmap displays the correlations between mathematics, reading, and writing scores. All three subjects show positive relationships with one another, with reading and writing showing a particularly strong correlation.

## Key Findings

1. Reading has the highest average score among the three subjects, while mathematics has the lowest average.

2. Mathematics scores are concentrated primarily in the middle-to-upper score ranges rather than being evenly distributed across the full range.

3. Reading and writing scores have a strong positive relationship, indicating that students who perform well in reading generally also perform well in writing.

4. Mathematics, reading, and writing scores are all positively correlated, suggesting that academic performance tends to be related across subjects.

5. The box plots show variation in performance across all three subjects, with students achieving scores across a broad range.

6. The visualizations reveal substantial differences in individual student performance, including both high-performing and lower-performing students.
